Qwen 2.5 3B Quantization Benchmark

Jan 2026

Benchmarked 4-bit NF4 (with double quantization) and 8-bit INT8 quantization of Qwen2.5-3B on an NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPU. NF4 reduced peak VRAM usage by 65% (5.84 GB to 2.02 GB), compressed weights 3.07x, doubled usable context length to 8,192 tokens at 13.7 tokens/s without OOM errors, and retained 67.0% accuracy on an MMLU subset (within 0.3 points of FP16).

LLM Quantization NF4 / INT8 Qwen2.5-3B
